ABOUT US

190 Studios is a service-oriented media production company providing creative, production, and post-production solutions across film, television, OTT, digital, advertising, and branded content. We work with filmmakers, production houses, agencies, brands, platforms, and organisations to turn ideas into well-executed visual content. From developing a concept to managing production and delivering the final film, we bring together the creative thinking, people, and production expertise required for each project. At 190 Studios, we believe great production is about more than getting the job done.
